# LLD 02 — C3 Quality control

**Goal:** check every keyframe and the capture as a whole, drop frames that would corrupt measurements, and report what went wrong with a fix the person capturing can act on (HLD §9).

```
FrameSet (C2) ──► run_qc ──► FrameSet (kept frames, rgb_ok set)
                         └─► QualityReport ──► outputs/<capture>/qc_report.{md,json}
                                           └─► quality_score (0–1, used by C12)
```

## Modules

| File | Responsibility |
|---|---|
| `scan/qc/metrics.py` | Per-frame measurements: sharpness, brightness, dark/blown-out share, depth validity, pitch, speed; tracking-jump detection on the full-rate path |
| `scan/qc/coverage.py` | Capture-level: was the floor / ceiling observed, camera height, share of upward views |
| `scan/qc/decide.py` | Drop rules, keep floor, issues with fixes, quality score |
| `scan/qc/report.py` | `QcConfig` (all thresholds), report types, Markdown/JSON writers |
| `scan/core/devices.py` | Device matrix lookup (generation, Pro, supported, LiDAR) |
| `scan/core/types.py` | Added `Trajectory` (every raw pose) and `Frame.rgb_ok` |

## Per-frame checks

| Check | Measure | Rule (default) |
|---|---|---|
| Blur | Laplacian variance at 640 px width, **relative to the median of ±4 neighbouring keyframes** | `< 0.4` → `rgb_ok = False`. LiDAR: frame kept (depth is from the sensor). Video/photo: frame dropped |
| Motion | Central difference over 0.1 s on the full-rate trajectory | `> 1.5 m/s` or `> 120 °/s` → drop |
| Exposure | Share of pixels `< 16` / `> 245` | `> 60 %` → drop |
| Depth | Share of valid depth pixels | `< 30 %` → drop |
| Tracking jump | Raw step speed `> 3 m/s` (events merged within 0.5 s) | Keyframes within 0.5 s → drop |

**Keep floor:** never keep fewer than `max(30, 50 %)` of the keyframes; the least-bad dropped frames are re-admitted and the capture gets `MANY_FRAMES_DROPPED`.

**Why blur is relative to neighbours:** a plain white wall is sharp but has little texture, so a capture-wide median marks it blurry. On the sample data the capture-wide rule dropped 65/238, 82/400 and 71/400 frames; the neighbour rule flags 2, 6 and 25, and visual checks confirmed the flagged frames are softer than their neighbours.

## Coverage

Depth maps of up to 80 kept frames are back-projected on an organised grid; surface normals come from neighbouring pixels. Only **horizontal** pixels (|normal·up| > 0.9) are used, so walls and furniture sides never count.

- **Floor:** horizontal points more than 0.5 m below the median camera height; floor height = densest 2 cm slab; area = occupied 10 cm cells within ±5 cm. Seen if area ≥ 2 m².
- **Ceiling:** horizontal points more than 2.0 m above the floor (or 0.6 m above the camera if no floor); same slab/area rule.

## Issues

| Code | Severity | Effect downstream |
|---|---|---|
| `FLOOR_NOT_SEEN` | error | No heights or areas possible |
| `TOO_FEW_FRAMES` | error | Capture unusable |
| `CEILING_NOT_SEEN` | warning | C7 reports `ceiling_height = null` with this reason |
| `TRACKING_JUMP`, `FAST_MOTION`, `LOW_LIGHT`, `SHORT_CAPTURE`, `MANY_FRAMES_DROPPED`, `MANY_BLURRY_FRAMES`, `DEVICE_UNSUPPORTED` | warning | Lower quality score → wider intervals |
| `DEVICE_UNKNOWN` | info | Device matrix check skipped |

Every issue carries a plain-English fix; these feed the capture protocol page. `scan qc` exits with code 1 when any error is present.

**Quality score:** `0.5 + 0.5 × (share of clean frames)`, multiplied by a penalty per issue (e.g. low light ×0.85). Ceiling coverage is not penalised: it removes a measurement rather than degrading the others. C12 calibrates how the score maps to interval width.

## Results on the sample captures (`--device "iPhone 15 Pro"`)

| Capture | Kept | Dropped | Blurry, kept for geometry | Floor | Ceiling | Camera height | Issues | Score |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| `single_room` | 217/238 | 21 fast motion | 2 | 16.3 m² | **not seen** | 1.39 m | CEILING_NOT_SEEN | 0.95 |
| `single_scan_floor_only` | 395/400 | 4 fast motion, 2 near jump | 6 | 43.4 m² | **not seen** | 1.40 m | TRACKING_JUMP (t = 113.6 s), CEILING_NOT_SEEN | 0.84 |
| `single_scan_with_ceiling` | 400/400 | — | 25 | 33.8 m² | **seen, 9.3 m²** | 1.47 m | none | 0.97 |

The ceiling verdicts match the earlier independent point-cloud analysis. QC takes 10–18 s per capture on the dev laptop (cached frames).

## Not handled here

- Mirrors and glass beyond low depth confidence → C4 (`mirror` / `windowpane` labels).
- People moving through the scene → multi-view consistency in C5/C7.
- Photo-tier overlap between photos → photo adapter.
- Coverage for the photo tier (no poses) is `unknown` until estimated geometry exists.
- Low light is judged on brightness only; iPhone auto-exposure can hide darkness as noise. A noise estimate is a possible later addition.
