# Morning capture checklist (2026-10-04)

Everything below is required by the brief's benchmark composition. About 45 minutes in total.
Put the files in Downloads, in the folders named below.

## 0. Before you start (5 min): stage damage in one bedroom

The brief needs one furnished room with staged damage of **two classes**. In Room_1 (or any bedroom):
- **Water stain:** a brown/yellow blotch on paper (tea or coffee works), about A4 size, taped to a wall at ~1.5 m.
- **Crack:** a thin, dark, jagged pencil/marker line ~40–60 cm long on white paper, taped to another wall.
- Measure each with the tape (stain: width × height; crack: length) and write it down.

## 1. Photos, every room (15 min) → `Downloads/photos_v2/<room>/`

Follow `docs/capture_protocol.md` exactly — it is what the testers will follow:
- **6–8 photos per room, never more than 8**, landscape, **1× lens (not 0.5×)**, chest height.
- Walk along the walls, 1–2 steps between photos, each photo sharing ~half its view with the previous one.
- Every wall in two photos from different spots, floor and ceiling lines visible.
- The door **square-on from inside the room**, whole frame in view — every room, incl. the hall side of each bedroom door.
- Stay inside the room; don't shoot through doorways. Folder names: `Hall`, `Kitchen`, `Room_1`, `Room_2`, `Room_3`.

## 2. Repeat room (5 min) → `Downloads/photos_repeat/Room_2/`

The repeatability gate needs **one room captured twice at the same tier**. After step 1, photograph
Room_2 again from scratch (new positions, same protocol) into a separate folder.
If the iPhone 17 Pro is still available: also scan Room_2 twice with Stray Scanner (best for the 1 cm gate).

## 3. Video (5 min) → `Downloads/video_v2/`

One walkthrough, **slowly**: one step per second, slow turns (5 s per quarter turn), tilt up to the
ceiling and down to the floor in every room, pause 2 s in each doorway. 4–6 minutes for the flat.
1080p 30 fps, "Most Compatible" format.

## 4. Tape measurements (15 min) → write in a note, send as text

Read the tape to the **millimetre**; write each number exactly as read.
- **Every door:** clear width (inside the frame) and height. Label by room and wall.
- **Every window:** width, height, sill height.
- Staged damage sizes (from step 0).
- If you have time: the hall's main wall lengths.
